Why did we choose this topic?

Looking up at the night sky, you might think space is quiet. Still, it isn’t. Above you, millions of things are happening, like planets drifting, stars exploding, and novae teeming with light. With this website, you can find out all there is to know about space, from the observable to the unobservable. From stars and planets, to the first voyage to the moon.
