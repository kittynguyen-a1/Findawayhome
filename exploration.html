Christina Koch holds the record for the longest single spaceflight for a woman, which was 328 days. She also participated in the first all-female space walk, which was another great achievement. She also took part in the Artemis II lunar mission, and became a powerful symbol and role model for young girls and future space explorers pursuing science, astronomy, and engineering.

The first human in space was Yuri Gagarin. He was a Soviet, and was launched into space in the heat of the Cold War, or Space Race. He was launched from the Baikonur Cosmodrome in the Kazakh SSR. Travelling on Vostok 1, Gagarin completed one orbit of Earth on 12 April 1961. The first human on the moon was Neil Armstrong. He was launched on July 16 1969 on Apollo 11, and landed on the moon on July 20 1969.  He is very famous for saying “One small step for man, one huge leap for mankind.” He returned to Earth on July 24 1969 and instantly became a hero.
